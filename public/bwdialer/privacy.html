Mogala – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

Mogala is a phone app made by Highsystem Botswana Proprietary Limited, Botswana (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what happens to it. In short: your contacts, call history and notes stay on your phone. We don't collect them, sell them or share them.

What the app uses on your phone, and why

  • Phone and SIM information (phone state, phone numbers, your SIM cards and their networks) – to place and receive calls and to pick the SIM on the same network as the number you dial (least-cost routing).
  • Contacts – to show names and photos, search your phone book, and add or edit contacts when you ask it to.
  • Call log – to show your call history, missed calls, favourites and frequently dialled numbers, and to delete entries when you ask it to.
  • Notifications and full-screen notifications – to show incoming calls, ongoing calls and missed calls.
  • Handwritten call notes, blocked numbers and settings – saved inside the app on your phone.

All of this is processed only on your phone. Mogala does not upload your contacts, call history, notes or phone numbers to us or to anyone else.

Calls

Calls are made and received through your mobile network, the same way as with your phone's built-in phone app. The app does not record calls.

Payments

  • App from Google Play: Premium subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; we don't receive your card details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription.
  • App from our website: Premium is paid with Orange Money. The app shows a device code made from a scrambled identifier of your phone. When you choose to send us your payment details by WhatsApp or SMS, we receive that code, your phone number and the payment reference, and use them only to issue and support your activation code.

Updates (website version only)

The version from our website checks a small file on our download server to see if a newer version is available. This sends no personal information; like any web request, the server sees your internet address. The Google Play version is updated by Google Play.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location access.

Keeping or deleting your data

Data the app saves (notes, favourites, blocked numbers, settings) stays on your phone until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Mogala. Your contacts and call history belong to your phone and are not removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Mogala is not directed at children under 13.
