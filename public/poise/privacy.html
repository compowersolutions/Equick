Poise – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

Poise: Neck Posture Coach is a phone app made by Highsystem Botswana Proprietary Limited, Botswana (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what happens to it. In short: everything Poise measures stays on your phone. We don't collect it, sell it or share it, and there is no account to create.

What the app uses on your phone, and why

  • Motion sensor (accelerometer) – to measure your phone's tilt and estimate how far your head is bent forward. It is read only while your screen is on.
  • Grip sensor (on phones that have one) – to tell whether a hand is holding the phone, so the app doesn't count time when the phone is lying on a table or seat.
  • Physical activity – Android's activity recognition, provided by Google Play services, tells the app when you are in a moving vehicle so that it pauses. Poise only uses the activity type (for example “in a vehicle” or “walking”); it does not access your location.
  • Notifications – to show posture warnings, break reminders and the ongoing “guarding your neck” status.
  • Display over other apps – optional. If you allow it, a full-screen reminder can appear when you ignore a posture warning. It never appears while you are in a vehicle or on a call.
  • Run in the background and start after restart – so posture guarding continues while you use other apps and after your phone reboots, if you choose.

What is stored, and where

Poise saves your settings and daily posture totals (time spent at each neck angle, average angle and number of reminders) only on your phone, for up to 30 days. None of it is uploaded to us or anyone else. Poise itself sends nothing over the internet.

Activity recognition is processed by Google Play services on your device under the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No camera or microphone access.
  • No location access.
  • No accounts, advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Health information

Poise is a wellness aid, not a medical device. Its neck angles and load figures are estimates from your phone's tilt and published research, and are not a diagnosis. If you have ongoing neck pain, numbness or weakness, please see a doctor or physiotherapist.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your posture history stays on your phone until you tap Settings → Reset statistics, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Poise. Uninstalling removes everything the app saved.

Children

Poise is not directed at children under 13.
